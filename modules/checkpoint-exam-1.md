# Exam

1. How many unique values are possible using a single binary digit?  
   Ans: 2

2. What allows digital devices to interconnect and transmit data?  
   Ans: Network

3. What data representation is used when a computer or network device
   is processing data?
   Ans: Binary

4. What is the order of bandwidth measurement from smallest to largest?  
   Ans: Kbps, Mbps, Gbps, Tbps

5. Which items are collectively referred to as network media?  
   Ans: Wires and radio waves

6. Which two devices are considered end devices?  
   Ans: Laptop and printer

7. Which three devices are considered intermediate devices in a
   network?  
   Ans: Wireless access point, router and switch

8. What are two types of wired high-speed internet connections?  
   Ans: DSL and cable

9. What are two methods typically used on a mobile device to provide
   internet connectivity?  
   Ans: Wi-Fi and cellular

10. In context of mobile devices, what does the term tethering
    involve?  
	Ans: Connecting a mobile device to another mobile device or
    computer to share a network connection.

11. Which technology is used in smartphones to pay terminal at a store
    in close distance?  
	Ans: NFC

12. What information may be requested when pairing over Bluetooth?  
	Ans: A PIN

13. Which wireless RF band do IEEE 802.11b/g devices use?  
	Ans: 2.4 GHz

14. Which feature is characteristic of MAC filtering in wireless
    networks?  
	Ans: it restricts computer access to a wireless network.

15. What two radio frequency bands are used in home wireless LANs?  
	Ans: 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz
